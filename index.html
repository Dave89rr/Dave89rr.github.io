D&D Combat Tracker

Visit site ↗

Live2026Personal projectSole developer

A live combat tracker my own D&D table plays with. The DM runs initiative, hit points and conditions from one screen; players see only what their characters would know, and every roll and hit reaches every open screen as it happens.

Next.js 15 · React 19 · TypeScript

Under the hood: a rules engine with no database calls, so it runs under over 2,000 automated tests. Postgres through Prisma with serializable transactions, an event log with undo, live updates over server-sent events, and D&D Beyond character import.
