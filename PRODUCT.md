## Platform

web

## Users

Recruiters and hiring managers screening for front-end or full-stack product roles; secondarily, small-business owners in the Atlanta area who might hire him for a site or internal tool. They skim fast, usually on a laptop between other candidates, sometimes on a phone from a LinkedIn link.

## Product Purpose

davidrivera.me is David Rivera's personal portfolio. It must make a visitor understand within seconds that he is a front-end-led full-stack developer (recruiters have misread him as backend-only), show real shipped interface work first, and get them to email him, book a 20-minute call, or open his résumé.

## Positioning

He came to software through the PRGM key on a TI-83 at twelve, then spent years in HR and operations jobs where he was the person stuck with bad software, and built tools to fix it. He designs for the people who have to use a thing every day, and he ships for real small businesses (a rental company, a property manager, a smoke and vape shop, earlier a repair chain), not only demo apps.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from a résumé, LinkedIn, Wellfound, or a direct referral. They compare him against other candidates' portfolios. The site is a single static page on GitHub Pages at davidrivera.me.

- Static HTML/CSS/JS, no framework; light and dark mode.
- Lead work, in page order: River Nest Properties (I1, live; sister company of his employer, not a client; Buildium listing sync + Basecamp lead routing), GamersAtlas (I2, personal project, gamersatlas.com; private repo), D&D Combat Tracker (I3, tracker.davidrivera.me; 2,300+ tests). Nimbus Smoke and Vape will take a lead slot once its site is live; Dispensed is off the page until its client launches.

## Brand Commitments

- Copy must survive an interviewer asking about any claim. No "hand-built", "wrote every line", or other claims that AI-assisted work would contradict. Don't label AI tool use on the page, per project or in a "how I work" line; it's a tool, like an editor. He discusses it openly in interviews and must be able to explain any project he shows.
- Avoid typefaces that read as AI-generated sites (Bricolage, Inter, Space Grotesk, DM Sans, Geist, Instrument, Fraunces, Manrope, Plus Jakarta).
- Only images that link somewhere lift on hover; an image opens the site in a new tab; "Visit site" sits beside the project name.
- No phone number, no floating Calendly badge.
