Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the personal calendar assistant application ("the App") operated by David Rivera handles information. The App is a private, single user tool built for David Rivera's own use. It is not offered to the public and does not create accounts for other users.

Information the App accesses

With your explicit consent through Google's standard OAuth sign in screen, the App accesses your Google Calendar data. This allows the App to read your events and to create, update, or delete events on your behalf. The App does not request access to any other Google services beyond Google Calendar.

How the information is used

Calendar data is used only to provide calendar features that you ask for, such as listing your schedule, checking availability, and adding or changing events. The data is not used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to managing your own calendar.

How the information is stored and shared

  • Access tokens are stored locally on the owner's own device and are used only to connect to the Google Calendar API.
  • The App does not sell, rent, or share your calendar data with any third party.
  • The App does not transfer your data to any server other than Google's own Calendar API, which is required for the App to function.
  • Google user data accessed by the App is not used to develop, improve, or train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

Data retention

The App does not maintain a separate long term database of your calendar events. Calendar information is read on demand from Google when needed. You can revoke the App's access at any time from your Google Account permissions page at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately ends the App's ability to access your data.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
